🪵 Scout Woodworking Modeler Guide

📱 Mobile Touch & Desktop Navigation: Single finger / Left-click to rotate camera. Two-finger pinch / Scroll wheel to zoom. Right-click / two-finger drag to pan across workshop floor.
🎯 Selecting Sub-Components: Tap or click directly on any piece of wood (e.g., individual skids, cleats, shelf decks, or side panels) to select it. A Cyan Outline highlights your active piece.
🔄 Moving & Rotating: Use W or tap Move to slide parts along 3D gizmo handles. Use E or tap Rotate to orient boards.
📐 Custom Resizing: Select any board, type new Width, Height, or Depth values in inches, and tap Apply New Size.
📋 Workshop Cut List: Tap Generate Parts / Cut List anytime to calculate quantities, exact cut dimensions, material species, and total board footage for trips to the lumber yard!
↩️ Undo & Redo: Made a mistake? Tap Undo or press ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z to step backwards.
